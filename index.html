05 / MY STORY

“I like asking what the data is actually telling us, then finding a practical way to act on it.”

I started with marketing and market research, then moved closer to the systems that make modern marketing measurable. That path has taken me through search, campaign execution, CRM communication and analytics.

I’m continuing to deepen the analytical side: stronger SQL, sharper business analysis and better ways to connect customer activity to commercial outcomes. I value work that is useful, measurable and honest about what the evidence can support.

Outside the work itself, I’m interested in learning systems, thoughtful technology and the small operational improvements that compound over time.
